Moss is an iPhone app to log what you eat, your weight, your body measurements, your sleep and your habits. It has widgets, an alarm (on iOS 26 or later) and actions for the Shortcuts app, and it can be linked with Ascent, our gym app, if you use it. It’s free and has no purchases or ads. 2. Who can use it

  • If you have or have had an eating disorder, talk to a professional before counting calories.

3. Not medical advice

  • Moss isn’t a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, including sleep disorders.
  • Calorie and macro targets, expenditure, body fat, sleep and the other numbers are estimates made with published formulas. They may not be accurate for you.
  • Moss limits what it suggests: it never goes below a calorie floor and never aims to lose more than 1% of your body weight per week.
  • What Moss says about your training (recovery, the performance you can expect, good days to try a personal record, or what to eat before and after training) is an estimate from your data, not an instruction. You decide how much to train and how much weight to lift: use good technique, and stop if something hurts.
